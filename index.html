Quick Start

Running the booth in four steps

1

Add your tracks

Drag the event's music files into the Track Library, or browse for them from your laptop. Nothing leaves this browser tab.

2

Load a deck

Load one track into Deck A and the next one into Deck B, ready to bring in.

3

Play and blend

Press play on Deck A. When it's time to switch songs, start Deck B and slide the crossfader across to bring it in smoothly.

4

Keep queuing

While one deck plays, load the next track into the other deck so the music never stops.
